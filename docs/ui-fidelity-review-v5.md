## Screen-zone comparison and implementation candidate

The official guide directly supports the macro arrangement below. On 27 Nov 2026, the production screenshot was compared with the 14 Jan 2017 Xian field screenshot. Both place the field scene in the center, portraits at left, a minimap at upper right, and status/chat/menu/date controls along the bottom. The Xian image shows two centered windows side by side with a small gap; an earlier note incorrectly described them as overlapping, so the layout and backlog have been corrected. The client removes the old navigation rail and permanent quest sidebar. Original 1199×675 Hearthcross, Mosswind Road and Amber Quarry illustrations give the field and route encounters distinct scenes and are included in the PWA app-shell cache. The desktop lower HUD is now grouped into three broad zones: overview, chat/field log, and client menu plus date/time. The production 1363 CSS-pixel viewport measures them at about 303/690/354 pixels (22.5%/51.2%/26.3% of available width); these are prototype layout measurements, not Xian dimensions. The 28px utility strip remains separate from the lower HUD. The contextual window frame uses original bevels, corner rivets, a double title edge, inner rail and midpoint fittings, but remains simpler than the historical screenshot. This broad-composition comparison does not establish current Xian client geometry or styling.

### Field-screen visual comparison

The 2017 Xian frame is a 1024×768 field view with portraits and minimap integrated into the scene, compact controls on the lower edge, and two dark, ornate windows side by side with a small gap. The current prototype uses original painted town and route backdrops, a left portrait rail, upper-right minimap, a compact three-zone lower HUD and contextual windows. Its utility strip has no project wordmark and measures 28px; the current lower HUD measures 118px high at the checked desktop viewport. The contextual window uses an original stepped inset bevel, corner rivets, layered title edge, fine inner rail and midpoint fittings, but remains simpler than the historical frame. At a 1363 CSS-pixel production viewport, windows measured 432px wide with a 13px gap, and the HUD zones measured about 303/690/354px. These measurements describe the prototype only. Clicking an exposed window focuses it, Escape closes only the front window, and the separate shop mode opens; no transaction was performed. This is a useful, version-scoped structural comparison, not a visual match to a known current Xian build. Further work should refine independent window ornament and verify HUD proportions against Xian evidence.

The V5.1 candidate uses original illustrations, portrait art, fictional content and prototype values. The general official battle guide specifies Ctrl+number group assignment and Esc → Withdraw, with an experience penalty for withdrawal within the first 20 seconds. It does not identify Xian or a client patch, so these are not claimed as current Xian mappings or timing, and the prototype does not implement that penalty. Exact Xian key bindings, selection gestures, battle timing and current-build field/window geometry remain unresolved.
